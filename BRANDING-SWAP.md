# Switching carriers

Alyssa is expected to move off Farmers. This site was built so that move is a
config edit, not a rebuild.

## The rule changed — read this

Content used to be generated by JavaScript from `config.js`, which made the
carrier swap a single-file edit. That was traded away deliberately for search
visibility: the served HTML was an empty shell, and her name, address and
phone only existed after JS ran. Most citation scrapers and directory
crawlers never run JS, and NAP data is exactly what they harvest.

**A carrier switch now touches two files.** `config.js` holds the carrier
block; `index.html` holds three carrier-domain email addresses.

Verify what's left at any time:

```bash
grep -rin "farmers" . --include="*.html" --include="*.css" --include="*.js"
```

Expect exactly two files: `assets/js/config.js` (the carrier block) and
`index.html` (email addresses only — in the team cards, the mailto links,
and the JSON-LD `email` fields). If a carrier name appears anywhere in body
copy, headings or CSS, that's a bug.

## The switch, start to finish

1. **Edit the `carrier` block** in `assets/js/config.js`:
   - `name` — display name used in body copy
   - `legalName` — the entity named in the footer
   - `eyebrow` — the line under her name, or `null` to hide it
   - `logo` — path to the new logo in `assets/brand/`, or `null`
   - `quoteUrl` — where "Get a quote" points
   - `disclosures` — **replace the array entirely.** Every carrier's required
     language is different. Do not adapt the old strings; get the new ones
     from the new carrier's compliance team.

2. **Drop the new logo** into `assets/brand/`.

3. **Replace the email addresses in `index.html`.** Find-and-replace
   `@farmersagent.com` with the new domain. It appears in the three team
   cards (link text and `mailto:` href) and in the JSON-LD block. Verify
   with `grep -c "farmersagent.com" index.html` — the count must reach zero.

4. **Check the license number** in `index.html` (footer) and the JSON-LD.
   Her personal license (#640903) follows her, but confirm it hasn't changed
   with the appointment.

5. **Bump `?v=`** on the three asset links in `index.html`.

6. Commit, push. Vercel redeploys automatically. No build step.

business card, listing and inbound reply pointing at it goes dead the day she
leaves. She needs a domain address — `alyssa@poppellinsurance.com` or

item to defer.

## Carrier compliance

Captive carriers usually require review of agent-created marketing sites.
Confirm Farmers' process before this goes public. If they require specific
disclosure language, it goes in `carrier.disclosures` — nowhere else.
